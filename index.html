About Me

I'm Hamad Alsheail, an artist based in Riyadh, working between words and photos.

Photography taught me to notice common things from different angles: a shepherd crossing a canyon at midday, a stranger walking through an empty tunnel or just observing the structure of a mountain.

Writing taught me to sit with what I observed a little longer — to process it and draw my personal reflection upon it. Chasing moments between landscaping adventures, to portraying life in the streets.

These days, that curiosity lives in Diwan Alshams ديوان الشمس — my space for practicing and experimenting across different art forms and medias.

Showcase

Wa'atha' 1447 H | وعثاء ١٤٤٧ هـ

Representation of the cumulative hardship of adventuring in nature. Escaping the urban fast-paced life to disconnect in the slow-paced nature.

A printed collage capturing daily portraits from left to right, showing the physical toll of the journey undertaken day after day in search of mental clarity — even if there is a physical tax — and highlighting the major stops along the trip in Saudi Arabia.

Wilderness

Open land, far from anything built.
What was here before us, and will remain after.

Animals

Existence, without the need to explain itself.
They live outside our idea of meaning.

Streets

Every corner holds a hundred unfinished stories.
Motion is the only constant worth capturing.

Humans

We move through places, and places move through us.
A trace is all anyone leaves behind.
