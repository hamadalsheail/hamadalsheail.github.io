Zine Maker

Eight pages, one sheet, one cut. Add a photo or a line of text to each page, then print and fold.

Paper

    Print advisor

    Add a photo to see which paper size prints it sharp.

    • Sharp: 300 PPI or more
    • Acceptable: 225–299 PPI, slight softness
    • Soft: under 225 PPI

    Effective PPI = photo pixels ÷ printed width of the photo on the page. Zooming in crops the photo, so it lowers PPI. Photos are never upscaled.

    How to make a zine

    1. Plan eight pages. A front cover, six inside pages and a back cover. One idea per page reads best.
    2. Fill the pages. Add a photo, a line of text, or both. Drag or use the arrows to change the order.
    3. Check the dots. Green prints sharp. Yellow or red: use a bigger photo, zoom out, or pick a smaller paper.
    4. Print and fold. Download, print single-sided at 100%, then follow the steps below.

    How to fold

    1. 1 Print the sheet single-sided, landscape, at 100%.

    2. 2 Fold in half the long way. Crease, then open.

    3. 3 Fold in half the short way, then fold both ends to the middle. Open: eight panels.

    4. 4 Fold in half the short way. Cut from the fold along the middle, one panel deep only.

    5. 5 Open, fold the long way, and push the ends together. The cut opens into a plus.

    6. 6 Fold the pages around so the cover is on top. Press flat. Done.
