The Najdi Algorithmالخوارزميّة النجديّة

{% include instagram.html url="https://www.instagram.com/reel/DdwvTUhIhg2/" %}

An artwork that depicts the emotions of the Saudi Najdi Ardah using design and generative algorithms, emulating the feeling of this national heritage and the elements it holds.

Light drawing
Draws the ornaments and shapes in the weave of the Daglah and the Shimagh.

Rhythm of the sword
A generative line that embodies the pattern of the sword's movement during the Ardah.
