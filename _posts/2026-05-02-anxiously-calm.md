My heart isn’t jumpy as ever but I hope this unfamiliar calmness stays forever.

Stars and constellations are vanishing from the sky’s keep,

Is the eternally living cosmos finally going to sleep?

Questions running through the mind, with no answers to find.

My sweaty palms became dry and I won’t bother to cry, but WHY!

Craving misery when life offered a bed of meadows to rest at ease.

How wild I become, rejecting domestication to panic ones again in peace.

Humming along the journey through joy and despair,

May the world burn at ease and fair…
